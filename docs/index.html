What I’m interested in

I’m interested in AI systems that know when they are unsure. That means calibrated classifiers, evaluation you can trust, and pipelines that pass hard cases to a stronger model or a person instead of guessing. Outside of that, I like turning physics into interactive software, and I’m interested in how people think, which is what my psychology training is about.

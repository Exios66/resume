Turn codes are 0 (halt), 1, 2, 4 and 8, or absolute headings N E S W. Color 0 turns right, matching the GollyGang convention.

The five colony scenarios

Each scenario builds the same map for every seed; the seed drives only the ants. A colony link (#m=colony&sc=…&x=…) restores the scenario and the seed, not walls or food painted during a run.

Scenario Grid (cells) Colonies × ants What it shows
Foraging trails 240 × 150 1 × 160 Three food patches at different distances; the nearest is stripped first, then the trail re-routes
Double bridge 200 × 130 1 × 150 Deneubourg’s 1990 experiment: a short and a long branch to the food, and the colony converges on the short one without any ant comparing them
Two rival colonies 240 × 150 2 × 130 One rich patch between two nests; fights leave alarm pheromone, which draws soldiers
Four-way war 260 × 170 4 × 100 Corner nests in a walled arena; territories form along trail networks, and colonies that lose their income starve
Maze 170 × 110 1 × 180 Food at the far end of a serpentine maze; once found, the trail is the colony’s memory of the route

Determinism checks

From a blank grid, 11,000 steps of the classic ant hash to the committed value 6219b3fb (FNV-1a). The same test passes on the Linux re-run, so it does not depend on the machine.

The colony tests check that a scenario and seed replay identically and that another seed differs, that ants forage and deliver food, that ants stay in bounds and out of walls in every scenario, and that rival colonies fight and raise soldiers. The colony simulation uses floating-point trigonometry, so these replays hold within one JavaScript engine, not across engines.

Reproduce

npm ci
npm test            # 108 unit tests in 11 files
npm run test:e2e    # builds the app, then 8 Playwright checks in Chromium

The benchmark is gated: RUN_BENCH=1 npx vitest run tests/bench.test.ts. On 11 November 2026, at commit 726a29a, all 108 unit tests passed. The full browser run passed 7 of 8 the first time: the start-view check failed once on a cold start. It passed when run alone and in a second full run, which passed 8 of 8.

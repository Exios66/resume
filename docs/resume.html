  • Built the scenario machinery: 89 needle/decoy pairs, 9 plot beats on a 0–3 temptation ladder, 28 condition cells with a seed-invariant truth spine, a persona-consistent NPC engine, and a scoring pipeline with deterministic metrics, paired LLM judges, and calibration gates.

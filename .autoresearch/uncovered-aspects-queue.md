# Follow-on research queue after chart evidence exploration

This leg builds on merged PR107/108/109. It covers seven table-generated charts, not every saved SVG, image or simulation. A static inventory of the current 43-page build found descriptions, canonical links and Open Graph images on every page, unique titles, and no missing local link or fragment destinations. That does not validate metadata semantics, external sources, runtime anchors, image dimensions or performance.

Every feature starts with its evaluation, then baseline, one small experiment, full gate on the exact candidate commit, screenshots, evidence and keep/discard record. Metric-blind ideas stay hypotheses until the evaluation is established. No broad zero-defect or full accessibility claim follows from the current score.
